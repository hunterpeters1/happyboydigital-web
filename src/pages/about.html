About

Hi, I'm Hunter.

Painter, builder, and someone who takes on more creative projects than is probably reasonable.

Self Portrait — the painted kind.

The short version

I'm Hunter Peters. I make paintings canvases and small graffiti style murals. When I'm not painting I'm usually learning how to do something else.

How I paint

My work leans toward bold color and exaggerated, expressive faces and places. Acrylic is my main medium. I love the way Impressionist artists can capture the emotion of their subject with quick color, I try to emulate that. I'm obsessed with Vincent VanGogh, he got it.

Happy Boy Atelier

Somewhere along the way I got tired of guessing at composition and perspective before committing paint to canvas, so I built Happy Boy Atelier — a small desktop app that lets me plan a piece digitally first: reference photos, perspective grids, lighting, all sorted out before the first brushstroke. It's free for anyone who wants it, and it's the reason "Digital" is in the name.

If any of that sounds like something you'd want on your own wall, have a look at my work or get in touch and we can talk about a painting or mural.
