FAQ

How do I buy a painting or a print?

Pick a piece from Work — each one has its price, print sizes, and step‑by‑step buying instructions on its own page. Payment is by Interac e‑Transfer, and I confirm by email once it's sent.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet — prints currently ship within Canada only. If you're outside Canada and really want something, email me and we'll see what's possible.

How long does a commission take?

Paintings can take about a month, murals can take the same but might require extra planning. I'll always confirm a timeline by email before starting any commission.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Email me a photo within [a few] days of delivery and I'll make it right — a replacement print or a refund, whichever makes sense.

How do I take care of my painting?

Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from humidity, and dust it occasionally with a soft, dry cloth. Acrylic paint holds up well, but it's still a painting — they're sensitive.

Can you paint something custom, not from the Work page?

That's exactly what a Painting or Mural Commission is — see the pricing above, or just email me with what you have in mind.
