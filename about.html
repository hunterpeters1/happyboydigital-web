The short version

I'm Hunter Peters. I make paintings — canvases, small graffiti style murals — and when I'm not painting I'm usually making something else. I'm obsessed with Vincent VanGogh, he got it.

How I paint

My work leans toward bold color and exaggerated, expressive faces, alongside more grounded studies like Roses and Sunny Field. Acrylic is my main medium. I love the way Impressionist artists can capture the emotion of their subject with quick color, I try to emulate that.

Happy Boy Atelier

Somewhere along the way I got tired of guessing at composition and perspective before committing paint to canvas, so I built Happy Boy Atelier — a small desktop app that lets me plan a piece digitally first: reference photos, perspective grids, lighting, all sorted out before the first brushstroke. It's free for anyone who wants it, and it's the reason "Digital" is in the name.

If any of that sounds like something you'd want on your own wall, have a look at the work or get in touch about a painting or mural.
